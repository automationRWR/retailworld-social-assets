# RWR Group Brand Guidelines (refreshed November 2026)

One reference for RWR Group and its four specialist brands: Retailworld, Hospoworld, RWR Health and RWR Construction. Built from the live brand websites (checked 24 Nov 2026), the approved social style (Notion: "Social Style: Fun & Trend-led") and the RWR Group guidelines.

**The live websites are the source of truth.** If anything here disagrees with the website, the website wins. Tell Marketing so this file can be updated.

## 2. Logo

- Use the official files. Never redraw a logo or set it in Poppins.
  - Full lockups with "Powered by People!": `logos/` in `automationRWR/hospoworld-social-assets` (6,000px+ PNGs for every brand).
  - Single brand logos: Google Drive, "Logo - Retailworld.png", "Logo - Hospoworld.png", "Logo - RWR Health.png", "Logo - RWR Construction.png".
- **On brand colour or a gradient:** white logo, the way every website header does it. To make a white version, set every pixel to white and keep the alpha channel.
- **On white:** the full-colour logo.
- Pair the logo with the "Powered by People!" mark where there's room: website headers and footers, covers, first and last slides.
- Keep clear space equal to the height of the rocket (Retailworld, Hospoworld), the heart (RWR Health) or the full stop (RWR Construction), and never less than 20px.
- Don't stretch, rotate, recolour (beyond white or full colour), outline, shadow, or put the logo on a busy photo.

## 4. Gradients: where they go and where they don't

| Use the brand gradient | Use flat colour only |
|---|---|
| Website hero and footer bands | Instagram and Facebook posts, carousels, reels, stories |
| Page headers that copy the website: Facebook and LinkedIn covers, email header bands, landing page heroes | Profile pictures and avatars (flat primary + white logo) |
| | Buttons, pills, icons, illustrations |

The gradient always runs light to the right, at the angle shown in the table. Never invent a new gradient, and never add glow, glass or blur effects.

## 5. Typography

**Poppins only**, with Arial as the fallback.

| Element | Website spec | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Eyebrow | Poppins Medium/SemiBold, ~12px, UPPERCASE, wide tracking, white on hero | e.g. "SPECIALIST RETAIL RECRUITMENT · AUSTRALIA & NEW ZEALAND" |
| H1 (hero) | Poppins Bold 700, 50px, white, tight leading | Two or three short lines |
| H2 (section) | Poppins Bold 700, ~36px, ink | |
| Body | Poppins Regular 400, 16px, `#222222` | Short paragraphs |
| Stat figures | Poppins Bold, brand colour | e.g. "6,800+" |
| Buttons | Poppins Medium, white on brand colour | |

**On social:** Poppins up to SemiBold (600). Mix sizes for depth: a light lead-in line ("Hot off the pass."), then a big headline with the last word in the brand colour ("Order **up.**").

## 6. Shapes, cards and buttons (from the websites)

- **Cards:** white, 14px radius, soft shadow (`0 1px 2px rgba(0,0,0,.04), 0 14px 36px -10px rgba(0,0,0,.16)`). Icon in a tint-coloured rounded square, figure in brand colour.
- **Primary button:** solid brand colour, white text.
- **Secondary action:** white circle arrow button with a label ("I'm hiring →"), or a white outline pill ("Register your CV →").
- **Header pill:** white 1.5–2px outline pill holding "Login | Sign up".
- **Search bar:** white rounded panel with labelled fields and a solid brand-colour Search button.
- Sections alternate: white, tint panel, light grey, then a solid brand-colour band, then the gradient footer.

## 7. Illustration and photography

- **Website heroes use flat vector illustrations.** Retailworld has a store assistant at the counter, Hospoworld a delivery scooter with a "Jobs / Candidates" box, RWR Health clinicians on a video call, and RWR Construction a site worker with a building. Use these for anything that copies the website (covers, banners). They are animated GIFs on the sites, so take a clean single frame.
- New illustrations should match: flat, rounded, solid colours with no gradients inside, the brand colour as the hero colour, and dark slate `#333B44` with skin tones for detail.
- **Photography:** real, warm, diverse people at work. Social crops often go black and white inside a circle, with an outline circle and a solid white circle as accents (see the Hospoworld "New to Facebook. Not to hospo." post).
- **RWR Construction:** never reuse a photo, and log every photo used in `photos/photo-log.md` in `rwr-construction-social-assets`.

Group voice: "Experts in people. Partners in growth." and "Powered by People!"

Rules for all brands:
- Short lines and full stops.
- No em dashes.
- No jargon ("leverage synergies", "talent solutions").
- Never use "kick your boss to the curb".
- Flag placeholder names, roles and reviews before anything goes live.
- Use reviews only with consent.

## 10. Pre-flight checklist

1. Correct brand, logo file and website (section 1).
2. One brand colour at full vibrancy. Gradient only where section 4 allows it.
3. Poppins throughout, with headings and sizes as in section 5.
4. Logo is white on colour and full colour on white, with clear space kept.
5. Cards, pills and buttons match the website (section 6).
6. Illustration is flat vector (website style), or photos are real and diverse.
7. Copy matches the brand tone, with no em dashes and no placeholders left in.
8. Put it next to the brand's homepage. If it doesn't look like it belongs there, it isn't finished.
