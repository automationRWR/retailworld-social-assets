# REOTY on Instagram, wave 1: the victory lap (October to December 2026)

Posted from the Retailworld Instagram account. The artwork carries REOTY branding only, as the brand
guidelines require, and the captions speak as the award. Three feed posts a week (Monday, Wednesday,
Friday), with stories in between. No emoji, no exclamation marks.

Tag each business's own Instagram handle where it says `[@handle]`. Tagged businesses tend to
reshare, and that is most of the reach in this wave.

Hashtags on every post: `#RetailEmployerOfTheYear #REOTY`

## Before posting

- Reels and animated posts are silent. Add a track from Instagram's commercial audio library when posting.
- Reel covers: pick a frame with the full headline. The grid crops reels to the middle 3:4, and every headline sits inside that.
- Quote pieces marked TEMPLATE carry sample text and a SAMPLE TEXT tag. They are rebuilt with the real quotes, not edited by hand.
- Badge spotted needs a real photo from a winner. Ask in the follow-up to the media kit.
